Get Help · Domestic Violence

Love should feel safe.

Abuse doesn't always leave bruises. It can look like jealousy, control, or someone checking your phone. MySGA is here to help young women spot the signs early, check in on each other, and know where to get help.

Get Help Now

Free, confidential, 24/7

Trained advocates can talk through what's happening and help you plan your next step. You don't have to be sure it's abuse to reach out.

For teens & young adults

love is respect

Support for dating relationships, including what's happening over text and social media.

  • Text LOVEIS to 22522
  • Call 1-866-331-9474
  • Chat at loveisrespect.org
For anyone in the U.S.

National Domestic Violence Hotline

Support for anyone affected by abuse, and connection to help where you live.

  • Call 1-800-799-SAFE (7233)
  • Text START to 88788
  • Chat at thehotline.org

In immediate danger? Call 911.

Know the Signs

Love shouldn't feel like this

In person

  • They get jealous of your friends or family time.
  • They tell you what to wear or who you can talk to.
  • They put you down, then say “I'm just playing.”
  • You feel scared to say no or to disagree.

Online & on your phone

  • They check your phone or demand your passwords.
  • They track your location “just to know you're safe.”
  • They pressure you to send photos.
  • They blow up your phone when you don't answer.
Healthy vs. Unhealthy

What love should feel like

Every relationship has ups and downs. But some things are never okay. Here's how to tell the difference.

HealthyUnhealthy
You both respect each other's phone and privacy.They go through your phone or demand your passwords.
You keep your friends, family, and activities.They make you choose between them and everyone else.
You can say no without fear.Saying no leads to anger, guilt trips, or threats.
Disagreements get talked through.Arguments end with yelling, name-calling, or pushing.
They support your goals and cheer you on.They put you down or make you feel small.
You feel safe being yourself.You feel like you're walking on eggshells.

If more of your relationship looks like the right column, you're not overreacting. Text LOVEIS to 22522 to talk it through with someone.

Check In on Your Sister

How to help a friend

Listen

Let her talk without judging or rushing her.

Believe her

Tell her it isn't her fault and she isn't alone.

Share a hotline

Give her the numbers above. Let her choose the next step.

Keep checking in

Stay close. One conversation is a start, not the end.
