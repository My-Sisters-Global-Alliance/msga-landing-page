Every Gift Moves Us Forward

My Sisters Global Alliance, Inc. is a Georgia nonprofit corporation, recognized by the IRS as tax-exempt under section 501(c)(3) and classified as a public charity under section 170(b)(1)(A)(vi). Contributions are tax deductible.

As an active, growing organization, every gift — no matter the size — directly supports the programs, resources, and relationships that help women and girls educate, equip, and empower themselves and their communities.
