Why We Check In

A Sister Reminds a Sister

Because Sisters Remind Sisters.

#CheckInSis is built on something simple: women reminding other women to pay attention to their bodies and take recommended screening and medical guidance seriously. One sister checks in with herself, then reaches out to check on another — and the circle keeps going.

Knowing your body and following recommended screening guidance doesn't replace a conversation with your doctor — it's the reason to start one.

MySGA does not diagnose, treat, or guarantee outcomes. Self-checks and reminders are not a substitute for professional screening. Please speak with a qualified healthcare provider about what's right for you.

Educate

Know the signs. Know your body. Know when to seek professional guidance.

  • Learn what's normal for your body so changes are easier to notice
  • Understand general screening guidance for your age and risk factors
National Breast Cancer Foundation → CDC: Breast Cancer →

Equip

Take the awareness you have and turn it into an appropriate next step.

  • When was my last recommended screening?
  • What changes should I bring to my provider's attention?
  • What screening schedule is right for my age and history?

Empower

Check yourself. Then check on her. Awareness becomes action the moment you reach out to another woman.
