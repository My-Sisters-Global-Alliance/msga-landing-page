A History of Service. A Future of Greater Impact.

Legacy in Action

MySGA is the next chapter of work that began years earlier through My Sisters Empowerment Network—practical service, trusted relationships, and a commitment to show up wherever help was needed.

Featured Stories

Showing up, in person

Ghana · 2025

Equipping an Orphanage

My Sisters Empowerment Network brought sanitary products, school supplies, and other essential items to an orphanage in Ghana. Romona and Afua—our sister in Ghana—stand with part of the supplies provided for the outreach.

Flint, Michigan · 2016

Sending a Semi-Truckload of Water

Moved by her family's personal connection to Flint—her husband is from the city—Romona organized a Central Georgia water drive through My Sisters. Her daughter, Jaylin, joined the effort, and local churches and schools partnered to collect enough bottled water to send a semi-truckload to families affected by the Flint water crisis.

Orlando, Florida · June 2016

Showing Up for Orlando

In the week following the Pulse nightclub shooting, Romona returned each day to stand with the Orlando community in prayer, comfort, and support.

Continuing Legacy

A commitment that carries forward

Macon, Georgia · February 10, 2015

Serving 13 Families in Macon

My Sisters prepared and provided dinner for 13 families staying at Ronald McDonald House in Macon while their children received medical care.

Macon, Georgia · Annual Participation

Supporting the Annual Domestic-Violence Walk

Three years ago, My Sisters began participating in the annual walk honoring LaSmockie Fountain and supporting people affected by domestic violence. MySGA will continue that commitment by volunteering at the October 31, 2026 walk. Photograph used with permission.

Macon, Georgia · 2024

Empower Your Next

After COVID-19 paused My Soul Is Satisfied, My Sisters Empowerment Network resumed its annual women's gathering in 2024 as the Empower Your Next Conference.

Rolling Fork, Mississippi · 2023

Responding to Rolling Fork

Following the March 2023 tornado, My Sisters Empowerment Network sent supplies and financial assistance to support recovery in Rolling Fork, Mississippi—a community connected to Romona through her family roots.

These photographs capture only part of the story. Much of the work happened before MySGA had formal programs, impact tracking, or someone assigned to document each moment. The priority was meeting the need in front of us.
