Privacy Policy

Effective August 25, 2026

My Sisters Global Alliance, Inc. ("MySGA") respects your privacy. This policy explains how we handle information you share through our website and connected services.

Information We Collect

We may collect your name, email address, phone number, mailing address, volunteer interests, donation details, and information you voluntarily provide when contacting us.

Our website and service providers may also collect limited technical information needed to operate and protect the site, such as browser type, device type, pages visited, and IP address.

How We Use Information

We use information to:

  • Respond to inquiries
  • Manage donations and donor acknowledgments
  • Communicate about volunteer and partnership opportunities
  • Send updates you requested
  • Maintain required organizational and financial records
  • Operate and protect our website

Donations and Email Services

Card donations are processed by Givebutter and its payment processor, Stripe. MySGA does not directly store complete payment-card information.

Zelle donations are processed through participating financial institutions. MySGA may receive the donor's name, payment amount, transaction details, and payment memo.

MySGA uses Brevo for certain email communications. You may unsubscribe from general updates at any time.

#CheckInSis Participation Counter

Our #CheckInSis campaign page includes a participation counter powered by Cloudflare, which already helps deliver our website. When you press “I Checked In,” we record only that one check-in occurred. We do not collect or store your name, contact information, IP address, or any health information. A small note is saved in your own browser so the same device isn't counted twice.

Third-Party Links

Our website contains links to external services, including social-media platforms and our donation system. MySGA does not control these websites and is not responsible for their privacy practices.

Sharing and Protection

MySGA does not sell or rent personal information. We may share limited information with trusted service providers when needed to operate our website, process donations, send communications, maintain records, or comply with the law.

We take reasonable steps to protect personal information, but no website or electronic transmission can be guaranteed completely secure.

Children's Privacy

This website is not intended to collect personal information directly from children under 13 without the involvement of a parent or legal guardian.

Your Choices

You may ask to review, correct, or delete information you provided unless MySGA must retain it for legal, tax, accounting, or recordkeeping purposes.
