Where We Focus

Our Work

This work isn't new — it's what we've been doing, person by person, for years. Today we organize it around three commitments, Educate, Equip, and Empower, through campaigns like #CheckInSis, our annual domestic violence memorial walk with LOADV, and our Homecoming to Prom dress drive. Reach out to learn how you can take part.

Every initiative we develop supports one or more of our three commitments: Educate, Equip and Empower. Together, these areas create pathways for women and girls to strengthen their lives, develop their leadership, and thrive.

In Memory

Remembering La’Smockie

Walking in her memory. Speaking for those who cannot.

La’Smockie Fountain was a loving mother and, in her family’s words, the life of the party—a people-person with a bright smile who loved to dance. After she lost her life to domestic violence in 2015, her family turned their grief into a mission: helping others recognize abuse and get out before it’s too late.

“She was such a lovely person, very beautiful, a great mother, and she absolutely loved to dance.”

Sharon Stafford, La’Smockie’s aunt

On Saturday, October 31, 2026, My Sisters walked alongside Loved Ones Against Domestic Violence (LOADV) in the 11th Annual Domestic Violence Awareness Memorial Walk in Macon. Together we honored La’Smockie and stood with everyone affected by domestic violence. This was our fourth year walking with LOADV.

Thank you to every sister, family member, partner, and neighbor who wore purple and walked with us. Domestic violence doesn’t end with October—keep sharing the hotlines on our Get Help page and keep checking in on each other.

Every October 26

Purple Light Day

Each year on October 26, LOADV invites the community to switch a porch light or window bulb to purple to stand with domestic violence survivors and honor La’Smockie Fountain. Mark your calendar for next year.
