Join the Work

Get Involved

There are many ways to support MySGA’s mission to educate, equip, and empower women and girls. You can volunteer your time, share your skills, connect us with community partners, sponsor an initiative, or help amplify our work. However you choose to participate, your involvement helps us build stronger programs, expand meaningful connections, and create lasting impact in the communities we serve.

Volunteer With Us

Our volunteers help with event support, awareness walks, and organizing donated goods. Right now that includes helping with our Homecoming to Prom dress drive. Tell us a little about your time and skills, and we'll connect you with current opportunities.

Email Us to Volunteer

Partner With Us

Churches, schools, businesses, and community organizations that share our mission — we want to hear from you. Partnership can mean hosting an initiative, referrals, in-kind support, or co-promotion.

Start a Partnership Conversation

Sponsor an Initiative

Several of our community initiatives welcome in-kind and event-specific sponsorship: supplies, meals, shirts, venues, and professional services.

Email Us About Sponsoring
Where You're Needed Right Now

Current Opportunities

Right now our Homecoming to Prom dress drive is collecting gently used homecoming and formal dresses, shoes, and accessories through January for 25 girls in Central Georgia. Throughout the year we also run awareness campaigns like #CheckInSis and our annual domestic violence memorial walk with LOADV, plus efforts such as a meal for families facing medical hardship, health-education gatherings, and classroom support. Email us and we'll share current ways to help.
