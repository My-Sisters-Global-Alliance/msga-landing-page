November 2026 · Now Collecting

Homecoming to Prom dress drive is underway

We're collecting gently used homecoming and formal dresses, shoes, and accessories through January so 25 girls in Central Georgia can each choose a dress for free at our Prom Pop-Up in March 2027. Businesses and service providers can also help with alterations, hair and makeup, photography, and gift cards.

October 31, 2026

We walked for La’Smockie

My Sisters walked with Loved Ones Against Domestic Violence (LOADV) in the 11th Annual Domestic Violence Awareness Memorial Walk in Macon, honoring La’Smockie Fountain and standing with everyone affected by domestic violence. It was our fourth year walking with LOADV.

October 2026

The #CheckInSis challenge for Breast Cancer Awareness Month

For Breast Cancer Awareness Month we launched #CheckInSis: check in with yourself, then reach out to check on another woman. We also opened our Honor Wall to celebrate the survivors, warriors, and loved ones who remind us to keep checking in.
