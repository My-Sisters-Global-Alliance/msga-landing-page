Where We're Based

MySGA — My Sisters Global Alliance — is based in Macon, Georgia, and serves women and girls through local, national, and global relationships.

How Can We Help?

Reach the right person faster

Pick the topic that fits and your email will open with the subject filled in, so it reaches the right person on our team.

Seeking Support

Looking for mentorship, leadership development, or someone to talk to about what you're facing? Tell us a little about your situation and we'll share the programs and resources that fit.

Email About Support

Donations & Tax Receipts

Questions about a gift, a Zelle donation, or a receipt for your taxes? Include your name, the date, and the amount so we can find your gift quickly.

Email About a Donation

Homecoming to Prom Dresses

Have gently used homecoming or formal dresses, shoes, or accessories to give? Tell us what you have and where you're located, and we'll follow up with how to get them to us.

Email About Dresses

Volunteering

Want to help at events, awareness walks, or with organizing donated goods? Share your availability and skills, and we'll connect you with current opportunities.

Email to Volunteer

Partnerships & Sponsorships

Churches, schools, businesses, and community organizations can partner with us on drives, walks, and outreach, or sponsor supplies, meals, venues, and services.

Email About Partnering

Need help right now?

MySGA is not a crisis service, and email isn't monitored around the clock. If you're in immediate danger, call 911. For free, confidential help with an unhealthy or abusive relationship, text LOVEIS to 22522 or call the National Domestic Violence Hotline at 1-800-799-7233, any time. See more ways to get help →

What to Expect

A real person on our team reads every message and replies by email. If your note is about a specific program or event, include the name and any dates so we can answer in one reply. We never sell or rent your contact information; see our Privacy Policy.
